# Weekly Regulatory Change Briefing — Week of 2026-05-15 to 2026-05-21

_Run date: 2026-05-22. 10 items captured across 7 federal sources and 1 state / territory._

## Top of the week
- **FFIEC proposes first CAMELS overhaul in 30 years** — All federal banking and credit union supervisors are seeking comment on a rewritten Uniform Financial Institutions Rating System that shifts the focus of safety-and-soundness exams toward material financial risk. Comments due August 17, 2026. ([FFIEC, May 19](https://www.ffiec.gov/news/press-releases/2026/pr-05-19))
- **OCC finalizes preemption of state interest-on-escrow laws** — Two final rules, effective June 18, confirm that national banks need not pay interest on mortgage escrow balances and that state laws in 14 jurisdictions (including NY, CA, CT, MA, and MD) mandating such interest are federally preempted. ([OCC, May 15](https://occ.gov/news-issuances/news-releases/2026/nr-occ-2026-37.html))
- **NCUA and Fed propose new frameworks for stablecoin issuance and payment accounts** — NCUA issued an NPRM for credit union payment stablecoin issuer standards under the GENIUS Act (comment period July 17), while the Fed proposed a prefunded "payment account" at Reserve Banks for eligible non-bank payment institutions. ([NCUA](https://ncua.gov/newsroom/press-release/2026/ncua-announces-proposed-rule-permitted-payment-stablecoin-issuer-standards)) ([Fed](https://www.federalreserve.gov/newsevents/pressreleases/other20260520a.htm))

## Items (priority order)

### 1. [Priority 9] FFIEC Proposes First Revision to CAMELS Rating System Since 1996 — FFIEC, Federal
- **Type**: Exam Update / Proposed Guidance
- **Date**: 2026-05-19
- **Summary**: The FFIEC invited public comment on the first comprehensive revision to the Uniform Financial Institutions Rating System (CAMELS) in 30 years. The proposal retains the six-component structure but refocuses composite and component ratings on material financial risks, shifts supervision away from process-heavy checklists toward quantitative factors, and improves transparency of ratings. FFIEC Chair and Fed Vice Chair for Supervision Michelle Bowman described it as "a decisive shift toward transparency, quantitative factors, and predictability." Applies to all banks and credit unions supervised by FFIEC member agencies. Comments due August 17, 2026.
- **Link**: https://www.ffiec.gov/news/press-releases/2026/pr-05-19

### 2. [Priority 8] OCC Issues Two Final Rules: Preemption of State Interest-on-Escrow Laws — OCC, Federal
- **Type**: Final Rule
- **Date**: 2026-05-15 (effective 2026-06-18)
- **Summary**: OCC finalized two rules affirming that national banks and federal savings associations may decide for themselves whether and how much to pay interest on mortgage escrow accounts. Rule 1 amends Part 34 (real estate lending powers) to confirm this flexibility. Rule 2 is a formal preemption determination that federal law preempts state laws in 14 jurisdictions—California, Connecticut, Maine, Maryland, Massachusetts, Minnesota, New York, Oregon, Rhode Island, Utah, Vermont, Wisconsin, Guam, and the U.S. Virgin Islands—that require payment of interest on escrow funds. Effective June 18, 2026.
- **Link**: https://occ.gov/news-issuances/news-releases/2026/nr-occ-2026-37.html

### 3. [Priority 7] NCUA Proposes Rule for Permitted Payment Stablecoin Issuer Standards — NCUA, Federal
- **Type**: Proposed Rule
- **Date**: 2026-05-15
- **Summary**: NCUA issued an NPRM establishing operational and risk-management standards for credit unions seeking to become NCUA-licensed Permitted Payment Stablecoin Issuers (PPSIs) under the GENIUS Act. Standards align with those proposed for bank subsidiaries and cover capital, liquidity, AML/CFT, and reserve requirements. Credit unions holding or issuing stablecoins should review the proposal and evaluate licensing timelines. Comment period closes July 17, 2026.
- **Link**: https://ncua.gov/newsroom/press-release/2026/ncua-announces-proposed-rule-permitted-payment-stablecoin-issuer-standards

### 4. [Priority 7] Federal Reserve Requests Comment on Payment Account Proposal — Federal Reserve, Federal
- **Type**: Proposed Rule
- **Date**: 2026-05-20
- **Summary**: The Fed proposed a new account type—a "payment account"—at Federal Reserve Banks that eligible financial institutions (including non-bank payment service providers) could hold solely for clearing and settling their own payments. Key features: fully prefunded (no intraday credit or overdrafts), balance capped at the lesser of $500 million or 10% of total assets, not usable for correspondent banking or settling third-party payments. Banks that provide correspondent services to payment fintechs should review the proposal for potential operational impact. Comment deadline to be confirmed in the Federal Register notice.
- **Link**: https://www.federalreserve.gov/newsevents/pressreleases/other20260520a.htm

### 5. [Priority 6] OCC Advances Priority of Reducing Regulatory Burden for Community Banks — OCC, Federal
- **Type**: Guidance / News-Announcement
- **Date**: 2026-05-19
- **Summary**: OCC published a news release describing implemented and forthcoming deregulatory actions for community banks. Effective January 1, 2026, OCC guidance removed mandatory examination activities for CRA performance, fair lending, end-user derivatives, and trading from community bank exams; the OCC will now tailor exams to each bank's activities and material financial risks. OCC also updated CRA exam scheduling to give examiners greater frequency discretion. The release also referenced the OCC's separate proposal to raise the asset-size threshold for "community bank" classification, which drew divided comment from trade groups.
- **Link**: https://www.occ.gov/news-issuances/news-releases/2026/nr-occ-2026-38.html

### 6. [Priority 5] Federal Reserve Terminates Cease-and-Desist Orders Against UBS and Credit Suisse (Archegos) — Federal Reserve, Federal
- **Type**: Enforcement (Termination)
- **Date**: 2026-05-15 (effective 2026-05-12)
- **Summary**: The Fed terminated its July 2023 cease-and-desist orders against UBS Group AG, Credit Suisse AG, Credit Suisse Holdings (USA), Inc., and Credit Suisse AG (New York Branch) related to unsafe and unsound counterparty credit-risk management practices tied to Archegos Capital Management. UBS had paid approximately $268.5 million in civil penalties (part of a coordinated $387 million cross-jurisdictional action with FINMA and the Bank of England) and has remediated the identified deficiencies. Relevant for global banks and prime brokerage compliance functions.
- **Link**: https://www.federalreserve.gov/newsevents/pressreleases/enforcement20260515a.htm

### 7. [Priority 5] OCC Announces May 2026 Enforcement Actions — OCC, Federal
- **Type**: Enforcement
- **Date**: 2026-05-21
- **Summary**: OCC released its May 2026 enforcement actions. Key actions include: (1) a Consent Order against Community Federal Savings Bank (Woodhaven, NY) for material deficiencies in its BSA/AML compliance program; and (2) an Order Terminating the Formal Agreement with First National Bank of Williamson (Williamson, WV), dated November 2024. The BSA/AML consent order is a reminder of OCC's sustained scrutiny of AML program adequacy at community institutions.
- **Link**: https://www.occ.gov/news-issuances/news-releases/2026/nr-occ-2026-40.html

### 8. [Priority 4] FDIC Issues FIL-22-2026: Updated Q&As on Official Signs and Advertising Requirements — FDIC, Federal
- **Type**: Guidance / Manual Update
- **Date**: 2026-05-15
- **Summary**: FDIC issued updated questions and answers addressing implementation of its January 2026 amendments to Part 328, which revised FDIC sign requirements for bank websites, mobile applications, and ATMs. The Q&As respond to specific questions from banks, trade associations, technology vendors, and other stakeholders. All FDIC-insured banks with digital channels and ATMs should review the updated guidance to confirm compliance readiness.
- **Link**: https://www.fdic.gov/news/financial-institution-letters/2026/updated-questions-and-answers-regarding-fdic-official-signs

### 9. [Priority 4] California DFPI Orders Hermes Bitcoin to Cease All California Bitcoin ATM Operations — California DFPI, California
- **Type**: Enforcement
- **Date**: 2026-05-18 (deadline for shutdown: 2026-05-20)
- **Summary**: California DFPI announced a settlement agreement requiring Anh Management LLC (d/b/a Hermes Bitcoin), operator of 42 Bitcoin ATMs across Southern California, to cease all digital financial asset business in California immediately. Alleged violations include exceeding DFAL's $1,000/day cash transaction cap, failing to verify customer identity in thousands of transactions, and AML non-compliance. The settlement carries a suspended $9.9 million administrative penalty, payable if the company fails to comply. Relevant for banks and credit unions assessing relationships with digital asset kiosk operators and DFAL-licensed entities.
- **Link**: https://dfpi.ca.gov/news/monthly-bulletins/monthly-bulletin-may-2026/

### 10. [Priority 3] Federal Reserve Names Powell Chair Pro Tempore Pending Warsh Swearing-In — Federal Reserve, Federal
- **Type**: News-Announcement
- **Date**: 2026-05-15
- **Summary**: The Fed Board formally named Jerome H. Powell as chair pro tempore, effective upon expiration of his term, pending swearing-in of Kevin M. Warsh as the new chair. Vice Chair for Supervision Michelle Bowman and Governor Stephen Miran issued a separate statement supporting the interim designation while noting its unusual nature. No immediate change to Fed regulatory or monetary policy is anticipated during the transition period.
- **Link**: https://www.federalreserve.gov/newsevents/pressreleases/other20260515a.htm
