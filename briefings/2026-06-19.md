## Items (priority order)

### 1. [Priority 8] Joint NPRM: Customer Identification Program for Payment Stablecoin Issuers (GENIUS Act) — Federal Reserve / FinCEN / OCC / FDIC / NCUA, Federal

- **Type**: Proposed Rule
- **Date**: 2026-06-18
- **Summary**: Five federal agencies (Fed, FinCEN, OCC, FDIC, NCUA) jointly proposed a rule requiring permitted payment stablecoin issuers (PPSIs) to maintain customer identification programs (CIP/KYC) comparable to those already required for banks and credit unions, implementing AML provisions of the GENIUS Act (enacted July 2025). The proposal limits CIP obligations to direct-to-consumer PPSI services, preliminarily rejecting a broader "global" due-diligence requirement. Banks and credit unions with PPSI subsidiaries or stablecoin operations are directly affected; comments are due approximately 60 days after Federal Register publication (estimated mid-August 2026).
- **Link**: https://www.federalreserve.gov/newsevents/pressreleases/bcreg20260618a.htm

### 2. [Priority 6] SEC NPRM: Rescission of Regulation NMS Rules 611 and 610(e) — SEC, Federal

- **Type**: Proposed Rule
- **Date**: 2026-06-11 _(one day before this window; not previously reported)_
- **Summary**: The SEC proposed to rescind Rule 611 (the Order Protection / trade-through rule) and Rule 610(e) (prohibition on locked and crossed markets) from Regulation NMS—characterised by SEC Chairman Atkins as the most significant U.S. equity market structure change in two decades. If adopted, broker-dealers would no longer be prohibited from trading through better-priced exchange quotations. Banks with broker-dealer affiliates or institutional trading operations are directly affected. Comment deadline: August 17, 2026.
- **Link**: https://www.sec.gov/newsroom/press-releases/2026-54-sec-proposes-rescission-regulation-nms-rules-611-610e

### 3. [Priority 5] NACHA Risk Management Phase 2: Universal ACH Fraud Monitoring — NACHA, Federal

- **Type**: Final Rule (effective date)
- **Date**: 2026-06-19 (effective; practical banking-day date June 22, 2026, as June 19 is Juneteenth)
- **Summary**: Phase 2 of NACHA's multi-year fraud-monitoring rule package eliminates all origination-volume thresholds, requiring every RDFI, Originator, Third-Party Sender, and Third-Party Service Provider to implement risk-based processes designed to detect and prevent fraudulent ACH entries, including unauthorized credits. Phase 1 (March 2026) had already applied to larger participants (over 6 million entries annually). All ACH-active banks and credit unions must confirm they meet Phase 2 requirements immediately.
- **Link**: https://www.nacha.org/news/new-nacha-risk-management-rules-now-effect

### 4. [Priority 4] FOMC Statement: Rates Held at 3.5–3.75%; Hawkish Dot Plot; Warsh Announces Five Task Forces — Federal Reserve, Federal

- **Type**: News-Announcement
- **Date**: 2026-06-17
- **Summary**: The FOMC voted 12-0 to hold the federal funds rate target at 3.5–3.75% at Chair Warsh's inaugural meeting. The June Summary of Economic Projections shifted hawkish: 9 of 18 officials project at least one 25 bps hike before year-end, with median 2026 PCE inflation revised up to 3.6%. Warsh's communication overhaul shortened the post-meeting statement to 130 words and he withheld his own dot-plot projection. Separately, Warsh announced five new operational task forces to review Fed communications strategy, balance sheet management, the inflation targeting framework, and related matters. Implementation note effective June 18, 2026.
- **Link**: https://www.federalreserve.gov/newsevents/pressreleases/monetary20260617a.htm

### 5. [Priority 4] NCUA Final Rule: Vital Records Preservation (12 CFR 749) — NCUA, Federal

- **Type**: Final Rule
- **Date**: Published 2026-06-16; effective 2026-07-16
- **Summary**: NCUA updated its 50-year-old catastrophic-act records preservation rule (12 CFR 749), removing prescriptive Appendices A and B, updating definitions, and granting credit unions greater flexibility to determine the content of their vital records preservation logs rather than adhering to a prescribed list. Credit unions should review and update their records preservation programs before the July 16, 2026 effective date. The change is part of NCUA's broader deregulation initiative and reduces compliance burden for smaller credit unions.
- **Link**: https://ncua.gov/newsroom/press-release/2026/ncua-board-approves-final-rule-vital-records-preservation

### 6. [Priority 3] OCC Clarifies Filing Decision Standards (12 CFR 5.13) — OCC, Federal

- **Type**: Guidance
- **Date**: 2026-06-17
- **Summary**: The OCC issued a statement reaffirming that all filing decisions are governed by 12 CFR 5.13 and that the agency may approve, conditionally approve, deny, or return as materially deficient any application lacking sufficient information. The OCC emphasised timely action on all filings. The statement is intended to remind the public of existing, longstanding practice—no policy change—but is relevant for institutions planning mergers, acquisitions, de novo applications, or charter conversions.
- **Link**: https://www.occ.gov/news-issuances/news-releases/2026/nr-occ-2026-47.html

### 7. [Priority 3] OCC Reissues Minority Depository Institution Policy Statement — OCC, Federal

- **Type**: Guidance
- **Date**: 2026-06-16
- **Summary**: The OCC reissued its MDI policy statement (Bulletin 2026-26) to align the definition of a minority depository institution with FIRREA, consistent with Executive Order 14219. The update removes references to obsolete agency programs and structures and clarifies the conditions for MDI designation. Current MDI status is preserved for existing designees. Minimal operational impact beyond the 12 OCC-supervised MDIs, but relevant for community banks seeking or holding that designation.
- **Link**: https://www.occ.gov/news-issuances/bulletins/2026/bulletin-2026-26.html
