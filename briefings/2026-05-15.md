### 2. [Priority 5] FDIC Releases Staff Study on Deposit Flight at Spring 2023 Failed Banks — FDIC, Federal

- **Type**: Report
- **Date**: 2026-05-14
- **Summary**: "Dissecting Depositor Flight: An Analysis of the Spring 2023 Bank Failures" uses transaction-level deposit data from Silicon Valley Bank, Signature Bank, and First Republic to reconstruct day-by-day outflows. Key findings: depositor size and sophistication are the strongest predictors of run behavior; the top 0.5% of depositors controlled 62% of Signature's deposits, 50% of First Republic's, and 39% of SVB's; uninsured balances were 94% at SVB, 76% at Signature, and 74% at First Republic. SVB and Signature each lost more than half their deposits in ten calendar days. The study will directly inform supervisory expectations on uninsured deposit concentration, liquidity stress testing, and contingency funding plans at banks across all size categories.
- **Link**: <https://www.fdic.gov/news/press-releases/2026/fdic-releases-staff-study-deposit-flows-three-failed-banks-spring-2023>

### 3. [Priority 3] Federal Reserve Releases Senior Financial Officers Survey Results on Discount Window and Reserve Management — Federal Reserve, Federal

- **Type**: Report
- **Date**: 2026-05-14
- **Summary**: The Federal Reserve released results from two surveys of senior financial officers covering banks' views on discount window operating days and reserve balance management practices. Survey 1 (January–February 2026) targeted banks with median assets under $5 billion; Survey 2 (March 2026, co-administered with the New York Fed) covered banks holding approximately 75% of total banking system reserve balances. Results provide transparency on how institutions of different sizes approach liquidity cushions, use of Fed facilities, and near-term balance-sheet expectations. Banks should compare their practices against peers as discount window stigma remains an ongoing supervisory focus.
- **Link**: <https://www.federalreserve.gov/newsevents/pressreleases/other20260514a.htm>

### 4. [Priority 3] Illinois IDFPR Launches Unified Online Consumer Complaint Portal for Banking and Financial Institutions — Illinois IDFPR, Illinois

- **Type**: News-Announcement
- **Date**: 2026-05-13
- **Summary**: The Illinois Department of Financial and Professional Regulation (IDFPR) launched a new online submission portal that, for the first time, accepts complaints against institutions supervised by both the Division of Banking (DOB) and the Division of Financial Institutions (DFI). The portal creates a unified, more accessible consumer complaint intake process for Illinois residents. State-chartered banks and financial institutions regulated by IDFPR should ensure their compliance teams and customer-service staff are aware of the new channel, which may increase complaint volume and speed of regulatory notification.
- **Link**: <https://idfpr.illinois.gov/news/2026/idfpr-launches-new-online-portal-streamline-consumer-financial-complaints.html>
