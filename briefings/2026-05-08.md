## Items (priority order)

### 1. [Priority 9] CFPB Final Rule: Revised Section 1071 Small Business Lending Data Collection — CFPB, Federal
- **Type**: Final Rule
- **Date**: 2026-05-01
- **Summary**: The CFPB published in the Federal Register a substantially revised version of its 2023 Dodd-Frank Section 1071 small business lending data collection rule. Coverage now applies only to financial institutions that originated at least 1,000 covered small business credit transactions in each of the two preceding calendar years (down from a phased tiered approach). Farm Credit System lenders are explicitly excluded. The final rule narrows required data points, simplifies collection procedures, and sets the compliance date at January 1, 2028. Covered lenders must file their first Small Business Lending Application Register by June 1, 2029. The rule is effective June 30, 2026; the Bureau signals a good-faith grace period for the initial collection year.
- **Link**: https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2026/05/01/2026-08494/small-business-lending-under-the-equal-credit-opportunity-act-regulation-b

### 2. [Priority 5] NCUA Announces Eleventh Round of Deregulation Proposals — NCUA, Federal
- **Type**: Proposed Rule
- **Date**: 2026-05-06
- **Summary**: The NCUA Board proposed two rules as part of its ongoing Deregulation Project. First, it would raise the Depository Institution Management Interlocks Act (DIMIA) major assets prohibition threshold from $2.5 billion to $10 billion, limiting anticompetitive-interlock concerns to larger institutions. Second, it would streamline Part 741 share insurance regulations for federally insured state-chartered credit unions by removing sections that merely duplicate or cross-reference other NCUA rules. Comment periods are open; the Federal Register notice for the share insurance proposal was published May 7, 2026.
- **Link**: https://ncua.gov/newsroom/press-release/2026/ncua-announces-eleventh-round-deregulation-proposals

### 3. [Priority 4] FinCEN Issues Consolidated and Updated CDD FAQs — FinCEN, Federal
- **Type**: Guidance
- **Date**: 2026-05-06
- **Summary**: FinCEN re-issued its Customer Due Diligence FAQ guidance in a single consolidated document, merging three previously separate FAQ sets and updating answers to align with the Account Opening Exceptive Relief Order issued February 13, 2026 (FIN-2026-R001). Under the updated guidance, covered financial institutions are no longer required to re-identify and re-verify beneficial owners at each new account opening for existing legal entity customers; verification is now relationship-based (at first account opening or upon a reason to doubt existing information or as required by the institution's risk-based monitoring procedures). Adoption of the exceptive relief remains at each institution's discretion.
- **Link**: https://www.fincen.gov/resources/statutes-and-regulations/cdd-rule-faqs

### 4. [Priority 4] California DFPI Monthly Bulletin: Digital Financial Assets Law License Deadline Approaching — CA DFPI, California
- **Type**: Guidance
- **Date**: 2026-05-01
- **Summary**: California DFPI's May 2026 Monthly Bulletin reminds covered entities that by July 1, 2026, any person offering digital-asset activities (crypto exchanges, crypto custodians, crypto kiosks) for California residents must hold a Digital Financial Assets Law (DFAL) license, have applied for one, or qualify for an exemption. State-chartered banks and credit unions with digital asset programs should confirm their exemption status or filing. The bulletin also notes that DFPI obtained its first administrative decision affirming California Consumer Financial Protection Law jurisdiction over debt-settlement and credit-repair services.
- **Link**: https://dfpi.ca.gov/news/monthly-bulletins/monthly-bulletin-may-2026/

### 5. [Priority 2] Federal Banking Agencies Issue Annual Host State Loan-to-Deposit Ratios — Federal Reserve / FDIC / OCC, Federal
- **Type**: News-Announcement
- **Date**: 2026-05-01
- **Summary**: The Federal Reserve, FDIC, and OCC jointly released the 2026 annual host state loan-to-deposit ratios as required under the Riegle-Neal Interstate Banking and Branching Efficiency Act. Ratios compare total loans in each state to total deposits held by all banks operating in that state; they replace those issued in May 2025. Bankers use these ratios to determine compliance with the law's prohibition on acquiring branches primarily to gather deposits outside the host state.
- **Link**: https://www.federalreserve.gov/newsevents/pressreleases/bcreg20260501a.htm

### 6. [Priority 2] Federal Reserve Vice Chair Bowman Speech: Artificial Intelligence in the Financial System — Federal Reserve, Federal
- **Type**: News-Announcement
- **Date**: 2026-05-01
- **Summary**: Vice Chair for Supervision Michelle Bowman addressed the FSOC Artificial Intelligence Series Roundtable on Cybersecurity and Risk Management, discussing the Fed's supervisory approach to AI in banking. She noted that the Fed, OCC, and FDIC recently amended model risk management guidance to clarify it does not apply to generative or agentic AI, which is instead governed by existing risk-management and governance frameworks. She raised cybersecurity concerns associated with dual-use AI models capable of identifying vulnerabilities.
- **Link**: https://www.federalreserve.gov/newsevents/speech/bowman20260501a.htm
